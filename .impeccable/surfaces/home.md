# Homepage — Kubo Web

## Intent and scope

Present an independent digital studio and verified work, then invite a project conversation on WhatsApp. This brief records the implemented homepage at `/`; it does not prescribe the composition of every internal route. Product facts and pricing remain governed by `PRODUCT.md` and the source content.

## Visual authority

User references: [Saleshook AI](https://saleshookai-landing-page-2-v1.21st.app/) and [Qronos](https://qronos-ai-agent-scheduler-template-v1.21st.app/). Their influence is the dark editorial studio atmosphere, confident typography, geometric object, pale interlude, and deliberate pacing. The implemented Kubo homepage and root `DESIGN.md` now provide the concrete local vocabulary.

## Implemented expression

1. Fixed studio header and dark hero: left-aligned oversized statement, lavender serif italic close, compact explanation, WhatsApp action, and a large geometric wire sculpture to the right. A restrained caption and bottom metadata rule complete the scene. The sculpture consists of 46 twisting square contours rendered in SVG and marked decorative.
2. Verified work: Helena Ávila and Cristiano Ávila screenshots, with minimal browser strips and real case links. Unequal columns and a (90px) stagger make this a selected-work spread. On mobile the cards stack with no stagger.
3. Pale lilac service section: interactive service rows reveal specific deliverables, pricing context, WhatsApp quote action, and existing detail routes. Preserve established price distinctions and budget caveats.
4. Dark principles section, followed by a four-step interactive process. The changing process panel includes a large numeral, code orbit, tags, and output.
5. Native FAQ disclosures, then a lavender closing invitation and WhatsApp action. The dark footer ends with an oversized `kubo` wordmark and real contact links.

## Interaction and responsive commitments

Service and process tabs update content and support their documented keyboard navigation. FAQ disclosures work natively. WhatsApp actions use the existing contact helper and service-specific message where implemented. Case links, service links, footer routes, SEO, and tracking remain functional.

The mobile hero places the sculpture below the readable copy and actions. Major grids stack, the project exploration cue remains visible, and process tabs form two columns. Reduced motion disables decorative drift and transitions. Preserve the working compact header and its explicit mobile menu state.

## Migration boundary

Existing portfolio, service, studio, contact, maintenance, and FAQ routes remain available, but their previous visual treatments have not all been migrated. Extending this homepage world into those routes is separate work.
